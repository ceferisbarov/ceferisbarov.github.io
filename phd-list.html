US CS PhD Programs

← Back to homepage

A broad candidate list of US universities worth considering for a Computer Science PhD. It is not a ranking of these programs against each other. It's a wide net that candidates can narrow down based on their own research interests, advisor fit, and funding needs. An institution is included if it satisfies all four of the following:

  • R1Classified Research 1 ("Very High Spending and Doctorate Production") in the 2025 Carnegie Classification of Institutions of Higher Education.
  • CSRankingsTracked by CSRankings as a US computer science department.
  • IPEDSConferred at least one CS-related research doctorate between 2021 and 2023, per IPEDS completions data, counting IPEDS CIP 11 (Computer & Information Sciences) and CIP 14.09 (Computer Engineering), since some "Computer Science and Engineering" departments report their doctorate under the latter.
  • Top 500Ranked in the global top 500 by at least one of three major world university rankings. See the box below for which ones, and why we added this.

108 institutions meet all four criteria. The full methodology, data, and code are on GitHub.

Why we also require a global ranking

Research 1 status and a working CS PhD program show that a university does research and trains doctoral students. They don't show that its name still carries weight once a graduate leaves the department. Advisor reputation and subfield fit matter most for the day to day of a PhD, but the parent institution's overall standing keeps shaping first impressions long after graduation. Hiring committees outside computer science, industry recruiters skimming a resume, and immigration officers evaluating a degree rarely have time to look into a specific department, so they read the university's name as a stand-in for quality. A ranking floor keeps this list focused on programs backed by a name that keeps doing that work for its graduates.

An institution qualifies if it lands in the global top 500 of at least one of these three rankings. We used the latest edition available for each as of August 2026:

ARWU (Shanghai Ranking) 2026 THE World University Rankings 2026 QS World University Rankings 2027

QS publishes its rankings roughly a year ahead of ARWU and THE, so its 2027 edition, not 2026, is the one currently live.

Notes

A few schools came up during methodology checks that aren't on the list, for reasons worth knowing about rather than treating as settled:

  • University of Arkansas clears the first three criteria only through a documented manual exception: its CS/CE PhD track is real but reported under a generic "Engineering, General" degree that IPEDS doesn't separate by field. It still misses the final list because it falls outside the global top 500 in ARWU, THE, and QS alike.
  • Boston College and University of Missouri-Kansas City both have real PhD programs that are simply too new to show up in the 2021-2023 completions data used here (Boston College's first cohort starts Fall 2026; UMKC's standalone CS PhD launched around 2025, replacing an older interdisciplinary track). Worth a look if you want a program's earliest cohorts.
  • Ohio University has a PhD, but it's a fused "Electrical Engineering and Computer Science" degree reported under Electrical Engineering's CIP code, which isn't included here to avoid also pulling in unrelated EE-only programs at other schools.
  • University of Mississippi advertises a CS track within an "Engineering Science" doctorate, but shows zero doctorates under any CS or computer-engineering CIP code across 2021-2023, so its output couldn't be confirmed.
