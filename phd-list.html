US CS PhD Programs

← Back to homepage

A broad candidate list of US universities worth considering for a Computer Science PhD. It is not a ranking of these programs against each other. It's a wide net that candidates can narrow down based on their own research interests, advisor fit, and funding needs. An institution is included if it satisfies all three of the following:

  • R1Classified Research 1 ("Very High Spending and Doctorate Production") in the 2025 Carnegie Classification of Institutions of Higher Education.
  • CSRankingsRanked in the top 100 US departments by CSRankings. See the box below for how that is measured, and why it replaced a general university ranking.
  • IPEDSConferred at least one CS-related research doctorate between 2021 and 2023, per IPEDS completions data, counting IPEDS CIP 11 (Computer & Information Sciences) and CIP 14.09 (Computer Engineering), since some "Computer Science and Engineering" departments report their doctorate under the latter.

97 institutions meet all three criteria. The full methodology, data, and code are on GitHub.

Why CSRankings, not a world university ranking

CSRankings ranks departments by faculty publications in each area's top venues, splitting credit among coauthors. No reputation surveys. The numbers here use the site's defaults: all 27 areas, top-tier venues, 2016 to 2026.

This list previously required the global top 500 of ARWU, THE, or QS instead. Those rank the university rather than the department, and weight medical research and prize winners. That floor cut Rensselaer, Worcester Polytechnic, Maryland Baltimore County, and William & Mary, all inside the CS top 100, while keeping schools past 150th in CS. Carnegie R1 already covers institutional research standing, so the ranking criterion is better spent on CS.

Notes

A few schools came up during methodology checks that aren't on the list, for reasons worth knowing about rather than treating as settled. Beyond these, a well-known university is usually absent simply because its CS department falls outside the US top 100:

  • University of Arkansas clears the R1 criterion, and the doctorate criterion only through a documented manual exception: its CS/CE PhD track is real but reported under a generic "Engineering, General" degree that IPEDS doesn't separate by field. It still misses the final list because it sits at roughly 135th among US departments in CSRankings, outside the top 100.
  • Boston College and University of Missouri-Kansas City both have real PhD programs that are simply too new to show up in the 2021-2023 completions data used here (Boston College's first cohort starts Fall 2026; UMKC's standalone CS PhD launched around 2025, replacing an older interdisciplinary track). Worth a look if you want a program's earliest cohorts.
  • Ohio University has a PhD, but it's a fused "Electrical Engineering and Computer Science" degree reported under Electrical Engineering's CIP code, which isn't included here to avoid also pulling in unrelated EE-only programs at other schools.
  • University of Mississippi advertises a CS track within an "Engineering Science" doctorate, but shows zero doctorates under any CS or computer-engineering CIP code across 2021-2023, so its output couldn't be confirmed.
