US CS PhD Programs

← Back to homepage

A broad candidate list of US universities worth considering for a Computer Science PhD. It is not a ranking. It's a wide net that candidates can narrow down based on their own research interests, advisor fit, and funding needs. An institution is included if it satisfies all three of the following:

  • Classified Research 1 ("Very High Spending and Doctorate Production") in the 2025 Carnegie Classification of Institutions of Higher Education.
  • Tracked by CSRankings as a US computer science department.
  • Conferred at least one CS-related research doctorate between 2021 and 2023, per IPEDS completions data, counting IPEDS CIP 11 (Computer & Information Sciences) and CIP 14.09 (Computer Engineering), since some "Computer Science and Engineering" departments report their doctorate under the latter.

161 institutions meet all three criteria, plus the University of Arkansas by a documented manual exception (its CS/CE PhD track is real but reported under a generic "Engineering, General" degree that IPEDS doesn't separate by field), for 162 in total. The full methodology, data, and code are on GitHub.

Notes

A few schools came up during methodology checks that aren't on the list, for reasons worth knowing about rather than treating as settled:

  • Boston College and University of Missouri-Kansas City both have real PhD programs that are simply too new to show up in the 2021-2023 completions data used here (Boston College's first cohort starts Fall 2026; UMKC's standalone CS PhD launched around 2025, replacing an older interdisciplinary track). Worth a look if you want a program's earliest cohorts.
  • Ohio University has a PhD, but it's a fused "Electrical Engineering and Computer Science" degree reported under Electrical Engineering's CIP code, which isn't included here to avoid also pulling in unrelated EE-only programs at other schools.
  • University of Mississippi advertises a CS track within an "Engineering Science" doctorate, but shows zero doctorates under any CS or computer-engineering CIP code across 2021-2023, so its output couldn't be confirmed.
